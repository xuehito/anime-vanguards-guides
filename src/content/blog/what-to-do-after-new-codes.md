New Anime Vanguards codes are best treated like a short resource spike, not a reason to reroll everything in sight. Copy the active list first, redeem on a fresh server if a code fails, then pause before spending.

The clean order is:

1. Check the [active codes page](/anime-vanguards/codes/) and redeem everything you qualify for.
2. Put Trait Rerolls only into a unit you already use or plan to build.
3. Check the [tier list](/anime-vanguards/tier-list/) before committing evolution mats.
4. Use the [trait reroll odds](/anime-vanguards/trait-reroll-odds/) page if you are chasing Monarch or another rare trait.

If a code gives Gems, decide whether you are close enough to pity before summoning. The [summon pity calculator](/anime-vanguards/summon-pity/) is the fastest way to avoid spending halfway and stopping with nothing guaranteed.

## Small rule

If the reward is limited and your account is early, save it until you know your main damage unit. Codes feel free, but rerolls are still one of the easiest ways to slow your progress.

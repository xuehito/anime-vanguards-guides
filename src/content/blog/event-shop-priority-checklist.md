Event shops are easiest when you decide your buy order before the last day. Most players should clear account-progress items before cosmetics or flexible extras.

Start with anything that directly improves your roster:

1. Limited evolution or event-specific materials.
2. Trait Rerolls if your main unit still needs a usable trait.
3. Gems or summon resources when you are near pity.
4. Gold and smaller currencies if they unlock an immediate upgrade.

Cosmetics are fine once your power path is covered. The mistake is buying them early, then needing one more upgrade material when the event timer is almost gone.

For a deeper route, use the [event guide](/anime-vanguards/event/) and pair it with the [farm Trait Rerolls guide](/anime-vanguards/farm-trait-rerolls/) when the event offers reroll value.

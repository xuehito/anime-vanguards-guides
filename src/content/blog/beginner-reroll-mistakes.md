Trait Rerolls are exciting because they can change a unit immediately. That also makes them easy to waste.

The first mistake is rerolling a placeholder unit. If you know you will replace it soon, keep the trait usable and move on. The second mistake is chasing perfect traits before your roster can clear the content that actually earns more resources.

A safer beginner rule:

1. Build a reliable story and farming team first.
2. Spend rerolls only on a unit listed as a real investment in the [tier list](/anime-vanguards/tier-list/).
3. Stop when you hit a good-enough trait unless you have a large reroll bank.
4. Save hard chasing for a main carry, not every unit in your inventory.

The [beginner guide](/anime-vanguards/beginner-guide/) covers the early path, and the [traits guide](/anime-vanguards/traits/) explains which traits are worth keeping.

Limited **events**, dungeons, boss rushes, and battle-pass style tracks are where veterans farm and where new players often burn stamina. This guide is a **playbook**, not a live patch note — always check in-game banners and the [official site](https://vanguards.gg/) for the current event name.

## What “event content” usually means

Depending on the patch, you may see:

- Time-limited stages / dungeons  
- Boss rush or gauntlet modes  
- Event currencies and shops  
- Limited units, skins, or familiars  
- Codes tied to the update ([codes list](/anime-vanguards/codes/))

Mechanics change; the **decision order** below stays useful.

## Play order (most accounts)

| Step | Action | Why |
|------|--------|-----|
| 1 | Redeem **new codes** | Free rerolls/tokens for the patch |
| 2 | Unlock **entry requirements** (level / story) | Many event stages gate early |
| 3 | Clear **easy free rewards** first | Guaranteed value, low wipe risk |
| 4 | Push **harder tiers** only with a stable team | See [tier list](/anime-vanguards/tier-list/) |
| 5 | Spend event currency on **account power** | Mats, rerolls, units > pure cosmetics if you are behind — see [farm RR](/anime-vanguards/farm-trait-rerolls/) for shop rates |

## Beginners: what to skip

If you are still in the [beginner path](/anime-vanguards/beginner-guide/):

- Do **not** empty gems on every event banner  
- Skip the hardest difficulty until you have one evolved + traited core  
- Grab free login / free track rewards even if you cannot finish the event  
- Use the event as **farm practice**, not FOMO shopping  

## Veterans: efficiency rules

1. Plan currency sinks **before** grinding endless stages.  
2. Evolve/trait the unit the event is designed around only if it stays good post-event ([evolve](/anime-vanguards/evolve/), [traits](/anime-vanguards/traits/)).  
3. Re-check codes on day 1 and day 2 of the patch — developers often drop follow-up codes.  
4. If the event unit is not SS/S for your holes, treat the banner as optional.

## FAQ

### Do events replace Story farming?

Usually not. Story and base modes still matter for levels and permanent unlocks. Events are **extra** efficiency or FOMO rewards.

### Should I save everything for the last day?

Risky. Servers, bugs, and personal schedule happen. Secure free and easy rewards early; push hard content when your team is ready.

### Where are exact stage guides?

Live stage gimmicks change often. Use this site for **priority and resource decisions**; use Discord / wiki for mechanic callouts on a specific boss the week it launches.

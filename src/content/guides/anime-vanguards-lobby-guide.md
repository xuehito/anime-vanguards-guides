Most systems live in the **lobby**, not in a match. This page is a **where to click** map. Numbers and shops change — use the [official Wiki](https://wiki.vanguards.gg/) for deep stats.

## Upgrades area

| Goal | Where / who | Notes |
|------|-------------|--------|

## Quick path (most accounts)

1. **Profile → Codes** — claim free RR.  
2. **Upgrades → Mandra** — trait your main DPS only.  
3. **Boss / Rift shops** — convert weekly tokens/shards into more RR.  
4. **Evolve** only after stats (and preferably trait) are acceptable.  
5. **Trade** only at 50+ and only if you accept trait wipe.

## FAQ

### I can’t find Mandra

Stay in the main **lobby**, look for the **Upgrades** zone labels. If UI moved after a patch, check the Wiki Trait Rerolls page for the current NPC name.

### Why did my trait disappear after trading?

By design on unit trades: **traits and pities are removed**. Plan before you send a Monarch unit.

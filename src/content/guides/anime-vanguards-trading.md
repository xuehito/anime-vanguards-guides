This page summarizes **hard rules** from [Wiki · Trading](https://wiki.vanguards.gg/Trading) — **not** a price list and **not** a marketplace.

Always confirm live in-game. Trading has been disabled after exploits before; if the UI is gone, this is reference only.

**Wiki states:** no official value list on the Wiki or promoted in Discord. Any sheet you see is **unofficial**.

<div class="callout callout--danger" id="pre-flight">

**Do not trade until you accept all of this**

1. Both accounts are **level 50+**.  
2. The unit is **actually tradable** (limited / unobtainable rules — still-on-banner limiteds may be locked).  
3. You accept **traits and pities are wiped** on unit trade (Monarch does **not** transfer). Stats / levels / ascensions **stay**.  
4. Only **Robux shop** Trait Rerolls are tradable — not codes / grind / most shop RR.  
5. There is **no official pricing** — third-party values are at your own risk.  
6. You are **not** paying or selling for Robux / IRL money through middles (ban + scam risk).  
7. You are not trading away your **only** clear core without a replacement plan.

If any line is “wait, what?” — stop. Read below or skip trade and [farm RR](/anime-vanguards/farm-trait-rerolls/) / [traits](/anime-vanguards/traits/) instead.

</div>

## Safety first

| Do | Don’t |
|----|--------|
| Trade only in official game UI | Middleman for Robux / IRL money |
| Screenshot the confirmation screen | Share passwords / account login |
| Both accounts **level 50+** | Trust “admin giveaway” DMs |
| Accept that **traits wipe** | Beg for free limiteds here |

We do **not** broker trades, buy units, or host RMT.

## Official rules (Wiki)

### Access

- Introduced in **Update 9.0**  
- Both players must be at least **level 50**

### What happens to units on trade (IMPORTANT)

| Kept | Removed |
|------|---------|
| **Stats, Levels, Ascensions** | **Traits and pities** |

Never trade assuming a **Monarch** survives. Pity on that unit is cleared per Wiki.

When trading stats at **S rank or above**, the trade UI shows the **% values** of those stats.

### Tradable items (Wiki list)

| Category | Detail |
|----------|--------|
| **Limited units** | Become unobtainable; still-on-banner may **not** trade until gone |
| **Giftable shop items** | Gamepasses, bundles, battlepasses, etc. |
| **Trait Rerolls (Robux only)** | Bought with Robux in the reroll shop |
| **Skins** | Limited or non-limited |
| **Familiars** | Including limited; Vanguard Familiars have special notes |
| **Unobtainable Memoria / mounts / evo items** | Specialty unobtainables as listed on Wiki |

### Trait Rerolls — what you can trade

| Type | Tradable? |
|------|-----------|
| Bought with **Robux** in the Trait Reroll shop | **Yes** (Wiki) |
| From **codes**, grinding, shops (Boss/Rift/etc.), bundles | **No** (FAQ / Trading notes) |

Farm free RR: [farm guide](/anime-vanguards/farm-trait-rerolls/) · [codes](/anime-vanguards/codes/).

## Value without official prices

The Wiki will **not** publish values. Relative value moves with banners, shiny events, and trade locks. Use third-party lists **at your own risk** — we will never host a value table here.

| Rough tier (thinking only) | Meaning |
|----------------------------|---------|
| Chase limited / meta | People still place it every run |
| Strong limited | Good, replaceable next update |
| Shiny flex | Taste + event timing |
| Dupes | Don’t overvalue |

## Common questions

### Do traded units keep traits?

**No** — **traits and pities are removed**. Stats/levels/ascensions stay.

### Can I trade my 500 code rerolls?

**No** if they came from codes/grind/shops. Only **Robux shop** RR.

### Should I trade my only DPS for “value”?

Usually **no** until you have a replacement. Power for clears beats screenshot WFL.

### Where do I open trade?

In the **lobby** when the system is on — [lobby guide](/anime-vanguards/lobby-guide/).

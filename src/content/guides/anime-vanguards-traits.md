## What traits do (simple version)

Each unit can roll a trait that modifies damage, utility, economy, or survivability. A strong unit with a bad trait often underperforms a slightly worse unit with a great trait.

Exact trait names and rates change with patches — always confirm in-game tooltips. This page is about **decision rules**, not a full data dump.

## When to spend free code rerolls

**Do spend soon if:**

- You already have a long-term DPS (banner SS/S or best A-tier you own)
- You are stuck on a stage and damage is the bottleneck
- You just redeemed a big Trait Reroll code and that unit is still default-trait

**Bank for a bit if:**

- You are about to pull a new banner unit you plan to main
- You only own early fillers (finish [beginner path](/anime-vanguards/beginner-guide/) first)
- A major balance update is announced in the next day or two

## Practical reroll loop

1. Pick **one** unit as the investment target.  
2. Reroll until the trait is **usable for your modes** (not perfection on day one).  
3. Stop and clear content — do not empty the whole stack chasing a 0.1% dream roll on a mid unit.  
4. Only resume rerolls when that unit is still your main after the next banner.

## Common mistakes

- Rerolling every new epic “just in case”
- Splitting 50 rerolls across five units equally
- Ignoring supports that amplify your DPS
- Spending all gems on summons, then having zero path to trait the unit you got

## FAQ

### Are traits permanent?

Traits stay on the unit until you reroll again (or use any reset the game provides). Treat good rolls as account progress.

### Should beginners trait early units?

Only if that unit will carry you for a long stretch. Otherwise level up, grab codes that need Level 30, and wait for a real main.

### Do traits matter more than evolution?

Both matter. Evolution unlocks kits and scaling; traits multiply what you already place. Skipping evolution to chase traits (or the reverse) both slow you down — do the cheap evolution steps on your main first when mats are available. See the [evolve guide](/anime-vanguards/evolve/).

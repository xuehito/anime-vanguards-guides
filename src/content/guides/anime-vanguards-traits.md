**Traits** are one of the biggest power levers after unit rarity. Free **Trait Rerolls** from [codes](/anime-vanguards/codes/) and [farm](/anime-vanguards/farm-trait-rerolls/) are premium — this guide is about **not wasting them**.

Monarch is **0.1%** with **pity 1,500** on Wiki — [**reroll calculator**](/anime-vanguards/trait-reroll-odds/).  
Where to click: [lobby · Mandra](/anime-vanguards/lobby-guide/).

## Spend decision tree

Work top to bottom. Stop at the first row that matches.

| If… | Then… |
|-----|--------|
| You plan to **trade the unit soon** | **Do not spend RR** — traits & pity **wipe on trade** ([trading pre-flight](/anime-vanguards/trading/#pre-flight)) |
| You have **no clear main DPS** (only fillers) | Bank RR. Finish [beginner](/anime-vanguards/beginner-guide/) or [returning](/anime-vanguards/returning-guide/) until one unit will stay for weeks |
| You have **one** unit you place every clear | Dump **almost all** free RR into that DPS until the trait is *usable*, not perfect |
| You have **two+ SS/S** you always run | Trait **clear-speed DPS first**, then the support you never bench |
| You are **1–2 days** from a banner you will main | Bank a stack; do not empty on a temp carry |
| You are chasing **Monarch only** on a mid unit | Stop. Fix the unit choice ([investment tier](/anime-vanguards/tier-list/)) before pity math |
| You still want Monarch on a real main | Use the [odds calculator](/anime-vanguards/trait-reroll-odds/) so pity **1,500** does not surprise you |

**Simple default:** one main → spend. Five projects → bank until one wins.

## What traits do (simple version)

Each unit can roll a trait that modifies damage, utility, economy, or survivability. A strong unit with a bad trait often underperforms a slightly worse unit with a great trait.

Exact names and rates change — confirm in-game. This page is **decision rules**, not a data dump ([Wiki · Traits](https://wiki.vanguards.gg/Traits)).

## Priority table (same idea, shorter)

| Priority | Target | Why |
|----------|--------|-----|
| 1 | **Main DPS** | Most of clear time sits here |
| 2 | Core **support** you always place | Multiplies the team |
| 3 | Secondary DPS / AoE | After #1 is “good enough” |
| Skip | Fillers you replace this week | High burn, low value |

## When to spend free code rerolls

**Spend soon if:** long-term DPS exists, damage is the bottleneck, big code just landed on a default-trait main.

**Bank if:** new banner main incoming, only early fillers, or a major balance patch is hours away.

## Practical reroll loop

1. Pick **one** investment target (tree above).  
2. Reroll at **Mandra** until the trait is usable for your modes.  
3. Stop and clear content — do not empty the stack chasing 0.1% on a mid unit.  
4. Resume only if that unit is still the main after the next banner.

## Common mistakes

- Rerolling every new epic “just in case”  
- Splitting 50 RR across five units  
- Ignoring supports that amp your DPS  
- Spending all gems on summons, then zero path to trait the pull  
- Traiting a unit you will trade tomorrow

## FAQ

### Are traits permanent?

Until you reroll again (or any reset the game provides).

### Should beginners trait early units?

Only if that unit carries a long stretch. Otherwise hit Level 30 codes, then wait for a real main.

### Do traits matter more than evolution?

Both. Cheap evo steps on a main first when mats allow — [evolve guide](/anime-vanguards/evolve/).

Left for weeks or months and the lobby looks like a new game? This page is a **first-session path** — not a full meta bible. Official data stays on the [Wiki](https://wiki.vanguards.gg/); we tell you **what to do in order**.

Brand new (never played)? Use the [beginner guide](/anime-vanguards/beginner-guide/) instead.

## 10-minute emergency path

Do this before summoning or trading:

1. **Redeem [codes](/anime-vanguards/codes/)** — free Trait Rerolls and currencies (often Level 30).  
2. **Open the current limited event** — claim **free track / login** only ([event playbook](/anime-vanguards/event/)).  
3. **Do not trade** until you read [trading pre-flight](/anime-vanguards/trading/#pre-flight) (Level 50, **traits wipe**, no official prices).  
4. Pick **one** unit you still place → plan RR with the [spend tree](/anime-vanguards/traits/#spend-decision-tree).  
5. If lost in the hub: [lobby map](/anime-vanguards/lobby-guide/) (Mandra = traits).

## Pick your “how long were you gone” track

| You left… | Focus this session | Skip for now |
|-----------|--------------------|--------------|
| **&lt; 2 weeks** | Codes + event free track + challenge RR | Full re-learn of every mode |
| **1–3 months** | Codes → free event → [farm top 3](/anime-vanguards/farm-trait-rerolls/) → trait **one** main | Spreading mats across old favorites |
| **6+ months / “what is Odyssey”** | Codes → Story/level gates if under 30–50 → free event → lobby tour → **one** clear team | Trading, every banner, Monarch pity panic |

Exact unit names flip every patch — use [investment tier](/anime-vanguards/tier-list/) + [Wiki Tier List](https://wiki.vanguards.gg/Tier_List) for names, not this checklist.

## What usually wastes a comeback session

- Summoning every banner before redeeming codes  
- Traiting three old units “for fun”  
- Trading a Monarch unit without knowing traits **delete**  
- Forcing hard event tiers with a year-old team  
- Chasing full Monarch pity on a unit you will replace this week

## FAQ

### Is my old limited still good?

Maybe. Check [Wiki Tier](https://wiki.vanguards.gg/Tier_List) and our [investment page](/anime-vanguards/tier-list/). If you only place it for nostalgia, do not dump new RR into it.

### Should I trade for the current meta?

Only after [pre-flight](/anime-vanguards/trading/#pre-flight). Power for *your* clears beats a screenshot trade.

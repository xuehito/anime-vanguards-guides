**Anime Vanguards** is a popular Roblox anime tower defense game. Summon units, place them on the path, evolve them, and clear stages across colliding anime worlds.

This site is an **unofficial** English guide hub focused on **session decisions** (not a full Wiki mirror):

- **Codes** — free Trait Rerolls, Gems, Memoria Shards, and more
- **Returning path** — back after a break in 10–30 minutes
- **Farm / odds / traits** — how to bank and spend RR without wasting them

## How to read results

| Output | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Pulls in budget** | Floor(gems ÷ cost per pull) |
| **Chance of ≥1 hit** | Independent pulls; hard pity can force 100% |
| **Expected pulls / gems** | Average cost to first hit with hard pity |
| **~50% / ~90%** | Rough bank size for those confidence levels |
| **Until pity** | Pity − progress, and gem cost for that stretch |

Hard pity model: if you never hit by pull `pity`, that pull is treated as guaranteed (same idea as the [trait calculator](/anime-vanguards/trait-reroll-odds/)).

## Practical rules

1. **Do not pull blind** — check [tier list](/anime-vanguards/tier-list/) first.  
2. **Bank for holes** — if your clear team is incomplete, prioritize utility over FOMO skins.  
3. **After you pull** — put free [code](/anime-vanguards/codes/) Trait Rerolls on the new main, not on fillers.  
4. **Re-check rates** every patch — banners change.

Unofficial planning tool. Drop rates and pity rules are owned by the game; always verify in-game before spending large gem banks.
